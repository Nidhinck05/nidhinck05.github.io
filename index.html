About me

A short introduction

I'm an IAM Architect at Flex, a global manufacturing company, with over 18 years of hands-on experience in Identity and Access Management.

My work spans securing on-prem Active Directory, hardening Microsoft Entra ID against industry benchmarks, building out privileged access with CyberArk Privilege Cloud, and federating identity with Okta. I've led complex hybrid identity programs, including corporate forest separations as part of a company divestiture.

I like turning security standards into practical, automated controls, often with PowerShell.
